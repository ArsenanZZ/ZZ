- Greek Key Inspired Fractal Meta-Structures -

Greek Key Inspired Fractal Meta-Structures blend the elegance of Greek key patterns with the intricate beauty of fractal geometry. These structures have good mechanical properties and can be used in a variety of applications, such as in architecture, engineering, and art.

- Sea Sponge Inspired Tubular Structures -

Tubular structures inspired by sea sponges have improved mechanical properties and are about twice as stiff as traditional structures. The structures are based on the glassy skeletons of sea sponges, which are stronger and lighter than current load-bearing structures.
