01 / Architected materials

Greek Key fractal lattices

Question: Can the geometry of a lattice improve deformation and energy absorption without changing the base material?

My work: I designed fractal lattice variants and used Abaqus to study the effects of geometry. Our team compared the behavior of printed specimens with simulations.

Result: In the published study’s tested configuration, the design absorbed 85% more energy than the honeycomb baseline. This is a comparison under the paper’s conditions, not a general property of Greek Key lattices.

02 / Bio-inspired structures

Sea-sponge-inspired tubes

Question: How does a sponge-inspired lattice change the response of a tube in different loading directions?

My work: I modeled printed tube geometries in Abaqus and compared them with physical tests under bending, radial, and axial loading. Looking at each mode separately kept the comparison grounded in what the structure actually needed to do.
