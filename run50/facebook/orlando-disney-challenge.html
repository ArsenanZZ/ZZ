Preface

US-1 East Coast: Miami to Orlando

🏝️Orlando, FL

Leaving Miami, our next stop is "Orlando"

Going all the way north, we did not take the highway, but chose the "slowest" route - US-1 Highway 1 East.

Outside the car window are large mangroves and lagoons, and the Indian River is very close.

Occasionally, you can still catch a glimpse of the resorts on the Atlantic Ocean, standing alone on the sandbank.

But when I was in the car, the pace was not that slow. My computer was spread out in the passenger seat, and I had to work on the "leading" job hunting PPT.

Later, he really jumped to Florida State, and I deserved a lot of credit.

Very good, the body is ready to run away, the soul continues to work overtime, move forward, Orlando.

★

Arriving in Orlando, the Dumb Challenge begins!

This time I came to Orlando not just for traveling.

In fact, as early as half a year ago, I had an “online battle” with runners from all over the world.

Laptops, iPads, mobile phones, desktops, all squatting——

The purpose is to grab this Disney’s “Dopey Challenge” running spot,

Disney holds a marathon event called "runDisney" in Orlando every January.

There is only one most cruel one: "Dopey Challenge", also called "Dopey Challenge"

Dopey is the silly one among the seven dwarfs:

The official meaning is: "Challenge your limits",

Actual meaning: "The challenge of running stupid".

💥 Four races in four days, a total of "48.6 miles" (approximately 78 kilometers):

- 🏃‍♂️ Day 1: 5K (3.1 miles)

- 🏃‍♂️ Day 2: 10K (6.2 miles)

- 🏃‍♂️ Day 3: Half Marathon (13.1 miles)

- 🏃‍♂️ Day 4: Full Marathon (26.2 miles)

You can get "6 medals" after finishing all races (1 medal per race + Goofy challenge + Dopey challenge).

In the evening, we came to the Disney ESPN Universal Sports Center,

This is Disney’s park dedicated to sporting events and activities, and is also the area for the runDisney Marathon Expo.

The Airbnb I stayed in this time was also quite interesting. Each room has its own theme. Ours is the "Disney Villain" route:

There is a row of portraits of villains hanging on the wall, from Cruella and Ursula to all kinds of sinister smiling faces, which is very atmospheric;

There is a bowl of "poisoned" apples on the table, paired with a mirror shaped like a magic mirror. It seems like someone will say behind me at any time: "Mirror, mirror, who is the most handsome runner in the world?"

★★

Disney 5K｜Travel through the Window of the EPCOT World under the Lights at Night

On January 5th, the day of the Disney 5K, we had to get up before dawn.

It was around four o'clock in the morning and it was dark outside the window. Siqi specially drove me to the starting point, thinking that this starting time is really inhuman!

The Disneyland park is full of runners wearing fancy costumes, some wearing princess dresses, some wearing superhero costumes, and some directly wearing string lights, like moving Christmas trees...

Although my mind was not awake at that moment, I was already drawn into the festive atmosphere.

The music was already playing, and the host was cheering on the stage, surrounded by lights and colorful balls, which even diluted the coldness of the early morning.

At 5 o'clock sharp, the first set of fireworks took off. It was no exaggeration, almost like a New Year's Eve countdown.

The runners set off amidst cheers, and the atmosphere was particularly relaxed. It didn't look like a race, but more like a "large-scale theme night tour."

The starting point is located in the outer parking lot of EPCOT. The first kilometer is on the service channel in the back yard of the park, which is dark.

The first mile into the park, running into EPCOT's main park, you can see the huge "Spaceship Earth" sphere at a glance.

It is the landmark of EPCOT. Its surface is a geometric sphere made of triangular metal sheets. It is very shocking when lit up at night.

Then I ran around the Window of the World Lake and passed through the exhibition halls of various countries. From "Japan's five-story pagoda" to "China's Temple of Heaven" and "Norway's cabin", every country's pavilion is lit up, like a night tour around the world.

There are actors greeting you at the door of every place - I like the "Chaoyang Gate" of the China Pavilion the most. The red pillars and gold tiles are so bright and impressive. Running through the door feels like returning to Laoga.

After about two miles, I ran to the "Coco" statue. The golden Miguel was playing and singing with his guitar in his arms, and Dante next to him looked cheerful.

Although it was still dark, this section was particularly warm, like a scene in a movie traveling through the world of the dead.

Not long after going forward, we arrived at EPCOT's landmark - the huge sphere "Spaceship Earth".

The lights kept changing colors, and the whole ball was lit up by lights. The colors kept changing in the dark night, making it look like a future planet.

Although it was still dark, the entire park was very lively. Most people take pictures while running and are not in a hurry.

I also took a photo, but I didn’t dare to run too far because I would be pulled back by the staff.

It probably took me less than 2 hours to finish the 5K. When I crossed the finish line, it was still dark.

At the finish line, I got a "yellow Pluto medal" with a gold base and "5K" written on it. It was very Disney.

5 kilometers, I ran around in the dark, but I still felt pretty good. The "Dopey Challenge" appetizer tasted good.

Disney 10K｜Run through lakeside towns and the lively BoardWalk

The day after running the 5K, the morning routine was the same—wake up at 4 a.m. and start running at 5 a.m.

The moment we started running, it was still dark, and the words “10K” on the starting arch were particularly flashing in the light.

Accompanied by a wave of pink and purple fireworks, the sleepiness was instantly gone.

As you can see from the map, this journey is richer than the 5K route. In addition to EPCOT, it also runs to another park all the way.

——Disney's BoardWalk Resort, running along the boardwalk by the lake, the street lights reflected in the water, like a morning jog in a European seaside town,

Although it was still dark, everyone was quite happy, and there were still many cosplayers and runners dressed up in fancy clothes on the road.

The staff kept shouting “Good job runners!” “You’re amazing!”

I thought to myself, "Isn't it just 10 kilometers? What's the point?" The emotional value of the old and American is indeed sufficient.

At one point along the way, I even saw the Incredibles family taking a group photo together.

In the second half of the period, the sky began to light up slowly.

When we reached the finish line, it was already dawn, and "Minnie" stood next to the finish line to greet everyone.

Siqi has been waiting for me at the finish line for a long time, and we finally took a photo with the female Donald Duck (Daisy).

🎖️ After finishing the run, I received a medal, which was a "Kiki and Didi themed 10K medal".

It has an orange base, two little squirrels jumping up and high-fiving on the medal, and there is an acorn pattern on the strap, which is very nice!

★★★★★

Take a walk around Orlando｜Orlando Urban Trail

After running the 10K, we drove to an Indian restaurant in Orlando - "Ahmed Indian Restaurant"

This shop is hidden in an inconspicuous community. The sign at the door is a bit old-school, but as soon as you enter, you can smell the familiar smell of curry.

They have a buffet for lunch. There are not many dishes, but the taste is very good.

Curry chicken, butter chicken, and naan are all special meals, and the prices are reasonable.

After eating, we went to the "Orlando Urban Trail", which starts next to Lake Formosa.

This trail is a bit like a green slow-moving belt in the city. There are lakes, bridges, and street murals along the way.

There are also some postmodern sculptures that are hard to describe but quite interesting.

We also saw a wall of colorful robot graffiti that said “You Matter”.

He also took selfies with various poses and hovers in front of the murals on the train.

It got dark while we were walking. At an intersection, a train passed slowly not far away. We waited for a while...

Then get back in the car and retreat, and the next day’s running Siqi will finally take place!

★★★★★★

Disney “Shrunk” Half Marathon｜A Fantasy Journey with a Key

In the next "half marathon", Siqi finally takes the stage!

Originally, this day was supposed to be the highlight moment of the "Disney Half Marathon" running into the Magic Kingdom park. As a result, the weather was unfavorable. The organizing committee made a temporary notice before the start of the race:

"There is a risk of thunderstorms in the forecast, and the original plan of 13.1 miles has been reduced to 7.1 miles."

In order not to suffer such a loss, I ran 5 kilometers before the start of the race to "make up for it."

It was still dark, so we started from the end of the team, and the rain started to fall while we were running.

The route that had already been shortened was forced to cut off another section later. By the time we reached the end, it was almost "half marathon, half marathon, half marathon".

As runners, we are actually not afraid of wind and rain, and we compete with my favorite Cincinnati "Flying Fish".

Siqi didn’t even enter a single Disney park!

🌧️ I thought the worst was over here, but it turned out that an even bigger "shock"/"surprise" was yet to come.

Never expected——

We arrived at the finish line in the rain, collected our medals, and ran to the parking lot to prepare to return to our Airbnb.

Touching his pocket: the key is gone.

It wasn’t left in a hotel or put in the car, it was—it was really gone.

The rental car has no backup and is a sea of cars.

The rain was getting heavier, my clothes were soaked, and I was cold. I stood on the edge of the parking lot, not knowing what to do.

We tried asking a volunteer, and she said she could help us.

Neither of us had high hopes. After all, tens of thousands of people were stepping on each other in this competition.

If the key was dropped at the starting point, it would have been trampled on long ago; if it was dropped halfway, it would be even worse, it would definitely be sucked away by the garbage truck!

Just when he was worried, the volunteer suddenly came over and said: "Someone really picked up a key over there at the starting point. What kind of car do you have?"

One question and one pair, the password is passed! It’s true!

She quickly contacted another volunteer through the intercom to help us deliver it.

After a while, a young lady with a face full of joy danced and handed the keys to us!

The key is covered in scratches, and you can tell at a glance that it has been subjected to a "high-quality human trampling" scene, but - it still works! You can also control the door remotely! My mood instantly went from the bottom to the sky!

💙 At this moment, the resentment in my heart about the shrinking route was suddenly relieved.

🌤️ After the rain in the afternoon, the sky cleared up, so I went out for a run by myself again and covered the remaining distance.

The competition is “shrunk”, I don’t want to be too “stuck” yet

I couldn't run into the garden, which was a bit of a loss.

But after experiencing "shrinking the route", "running in heavy rain", "lost the key", "lost and found",

This "roller coaster" experience is truly more exciting than any other "ride"!

Postscript

To be honest, I don’t want to talk about “life is a journey, regrets are also scenery”, nonsense that makes my mouth twitch and hiss.

But whoever is responsible for this matter will feel a little "thump" in his heart.

Many times, things are just like this shrinking half marathon:

You were clearly prepared ahead of time, but the rain fell as soon as it came, and the route was cut as soon as it came.

Just as I was catching my breath after running, the car keys were gone, and I stood in the rain in a daze.

Just like my colleague, a tornado tree smashed his car two days ago, and everyone asked him how he could still laugh.

He said: "If you want to live a passable life, you have to live it cheerfully."

It sounds weird, but it makes sense.

Just like our key, we had no hope at all, but the volunteers in raincoats jumped around and returned it.

Anyway——

As soon as it comes, it’s done!

The next "Full Marathon"! Keep up the good work!
