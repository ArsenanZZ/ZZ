A warm New Year's trip to America's southern edge: short sleeves, beaches, palms, and a Florida prelude before Disney Marathon.

丨Florida丨

-Key West-

🌴 Little Hainan in the United States, I don’t know how cold it is during New Year’s Eve.

🌊 The sea breeze blows ashore, the joy never stops!

Preface

I haven’t updated in a year, so I can’t write anymore.

🏝️ Key West, FL

🫠 Every time I want to write, I am persuaded to stop. Why?

Because the next destination is Florida, which has a large amount of content, high prices, and a long trip, so it is quite difficult to write...

😴 Besides, instead of writing a travel diary, I actually want to lie down for a while.

As a result, the whole year of 2024 has passed by this time. Looking back, the tweet update = 0, which is very shameful (comfortable).

🎊 2025 is here, new year and new KPIs!

This year I plan to publish at least one article every month, and try to clear out the inventory by the end of the year, otherwise this travelogue will be postponed until 2030...

🌍 Back at the end of 2023, we went to Key West, the southernmost tip of Florida, to welcome 2024!

📺 The first time I heard about this place was in the documentary "Couples"

It's about the "Conch Republic" - when Key West actually "went war" with the United States with bread and toilet paper, and then surrendered in seconds... This god-level event piqued my interest instantly! 😂

📍 Key West, the Chinese name is "Key West", also called "Key West"

But I'm not very good at this literary name. Maybe it's because I'm getting older and have become less and less interested in fresh and fresh names. I'll be done with it if it's rough.

💀 However, its Spanish name "Cayo Hueso" is very touching!

It means "an island made of human bones" - it is said that the Indians buried their ancestors here. This place may be a mixture of white coral and ancient bones...

😏 This Latin American dark style is much cooler than the "petty bourgeoisie island resort", so I say, Key West, go for it!

★

Landed in Miami, Florida does not support idlers

💨 Landing in Miami in the evening, I stepped into the tropical crowds of people at the end of the year. I felt like Northeasterners rushing into Hainan to spend the winter. The airport was full of travelers in vacation mode! 🏝️

🚕 I called an Uber to go to Airbnb, but ended up waiting for half an hour outside the sweltering airport passage. The driver took advantage of the traffic and walked up to us, as if he had just experienced a street survival battle...

⚡ After getting in the car, the driver drove straight on the highway and saw the traffic jam in front of him. He jumped into the emergency lane and fought his way out of the siege. I said "WOC! WOC!" on the spot: Florida really doesn't support idle people!

🏠 The Airbnb is close to Lake Mahar, with greenery everywhere and a tropical atmosphere. We take a breath under the coconut trees and face the challenge of renting a car tomorrow.

🚗 The next day, a lady who only spoke Spanish delivered the car to us. We took out the translation software and inputted it frantically, relying on AI to make the handover smooth.

🗣️ It can be seen that the Spanish level of 47 still has a lot of room for improvement... 😂

🔎 With a car, I don’t have to worry about anything. All I need is a mobile phone holder for navigation. As soon as I entered Walmart, I discovered that 70% of the people here speak Spanish!

🍽️ Everything is ready, let’s go to the Golden Corral to worship the five internal organs temple, and then the trip officially begins! Destination: Key West! 🚗💨🌴

★★

Mysterious Night Road to Key West

🚗💨 US-1 is the most beautiful self-driving route in the world during the day, and a dark and unknown adventure at night...

🌑 The scenery along the way? It doesn’t exist because you can’t see it! Except for the few meters illuminated by the car lights, it all depends on imagination to complete the magnificent picture of the Atlantic Ocean🌊

🌴 Before dark, we stopped at Far Beach and watched a magical sunset! This is located in John Pennekamp Coral Reef State Park, one of the most famous state parks in the Florida Keys.

🔥 The pink and purple glow reflected on the water is dreamy and peaceful, definitely worth the price of admission! Although I missed the daytime sea view on Highway 1, the sunset + drone aerial photography here is completely another ultimate experience!

🚁 I flew a drone and photographed the "Fire Cloud" on the water. I once wondered: Is it reality or a movie special effect? 🌅

🌙 In the evening, the staff will explain Florida’s natural knowledge to the children here.

🏠 Arrive at the B&B late at night, tired from the journey and the beautiful movies in the camera, looking forward to officially exploring Key West tomorrow! 🌟

★★★

Key West New Year’s Eve party record: sunset, warships, cats

🏝️ Our Airbnb is located between Stock Island and Cow Key, which is considered a suburb of Key West. The small white houses are beautiful and the island has strong sunshine. It is quite pleasant to live there.

🚗 Follow the Overseas Highway, all the way through New Town and straight to Old Town. There are so many tourists and the parking fee is so expensive!

🅿️ After all my efforts, I finally found a parking lot near Fort Zachary Taylor Historic State Park. The environment is good and it’s free! 🐶🎉

🚶‍♂️ Parked the car and started exploring on foot along Truman Waterfront Park. First, we saw a super handsome warship - "USCGC Ingham Maritime Museum"!

⚓ This ship used to be the Coast Guard patrol ship USCGC Ingham (WHEC-35). It was later decommissioned and turned into a museum. It’s full of history!

🌊 The scenery on the coastline is excellent, the Florida sun is very comfortable, seabirds stand lazily on wooden piles, yachts are moored quietly on the water, everything is slow and leisurely.

🚶‍♂️ Key West is not big. After a short walk, we arrived at "Southernmost Point", which is known as "the end of the world in the continental United States" and is one of the most visited landmarks.

📸 But "ends of the world" all over the world seem to have the same routine - a big rock + a long queue for taking pictures. This is the case in Sanya, and it is the same here.

🍹The stone says “90” – meaning it’s only 90 miles from Cuba! But there were too many people, so we took a symbolic photo and quickly left.

🌬️ When walking to the nearby "Emma Carrero Cates Pier", the wind was super strong! The aroma of the food at Seaside Cafe is so enticing that 47’s hat will blow off! 😱

👀 Fortunately, a kind person with quick eyes and quick hands caught the hat and returned it to its original owner!

⌛ Look at the watch, it’s almost 2 o’clock, and my stomach is growling with hunger. I wonder if I can find Chinese food on such a remote island in the Atlantic Ocean?

📍 After searching on Google, I found "China Garden West". American-style Chinese food is indeed everywhere!

🥢 When we entered the restaurant, there were only us and 47 in the whole restaurant. The taste was not amazing, but it was better than filling us up.

📖 After eating, we went straight to the former home of Hemingway. This great writer moved to Key West in 1931 and wrote such classics as "The Old Man and the Sea", "The Snows of Kilimanjaro", and "The Green Hills of Africa".

💀 The inspiration for the book "The Old Man and the Sea" came from Key West. A fisherman went out to sea and had a fight with a big fish, but in the end he only dragged back a set of fish bones. It feels a bit realistic when you think about it.

🏡 This old house with yellow shutters and Spanish-style balcony was moved by Hemingway in 1931. At that time, he and his second wife Pauline moved in and stayed there for 8 years.

🔦 Opposite the former residence is the Key West Lighthouse. This light not only guides ships on the sea, but also guided countless drunk Hemingway.

🍺 Because he is a heavy drinker himself, he hangs out in Sloppy Joe’s Bar all year round. When he drinks to the point of unconsciousness, when he sees the lighthouse, he knows where his home is going.

🐱 But the biggest star of Hemingway House is the six-toed cat! This group of cats live leisurely in a two-story villa. They are very cute and not afraid of people at all. There are also the tombstones of Hemingway's four beloved cats in the yard!

🐾 There are more cats than people in this house, and there are more stories than cats. Romance, madness, and the smell of alcohol are the unique style of Key West.

🚗 After watching the cats, we picked up the car and drove to the southernmost point of Key West - Fort Zachary Taylor Beach, which is more private and has a wider view than Mallory Square and Southernmost Point!

🌊 The sea water is clear, and the sky is dyed into the color of burning clouds by the setting sun. This is definitely the second most beautiful sunset I have ever seen in my life, second only to the primitive wildness of Lingshui, Hainan!

📸 I originally wanted to take some selfies with the Hover drone, but as soon as I took off, I was stopped by the staff, so I quickly put it away and pretended like nothing happened. But at least some good photos were left behind.

🌃 As night falls, Key West enters New Year’s Eve carnival mode

🔥 Stop 1: Tiki House

The DJ was playing live upstairs, and the door was full of enthusiastic drunkards, making the atmosphere full!

🔥 Stop 2: Stumbled into the Bourbon St. Pub drag queen party

Drag queen Sushi sits in giant red high heels and is slowly lowered down to the highest LGBTQ+ party in Key West!

We strayed into the crowd and were unable to advance or retreat. In front of us were big high-heeled shoes dropped from the sky, and next to us was a fat dancing figure...

I once fell into the scene of social phobia. I am not suitable for such an atmosphere, but I also respect it.

🔥 Stop 3: Sloppy Joe’s Bar Official Countdown

The giant crystal ball counts down, and everyone holds up their mobile phones to record the New Year’s Eve moments!

2023 is over and 2024 is here. Looking back, the time in the past seems like just yesterday... but it has also come a long way.

🚶‍♂️ We walked back to the hostel in the dark, and Key West’s New Year’s Eve carnival was still going on.

🤣 Just before reaching the door, 47 completed the first fall of 2024!

🌟 Falling starts in the new year, everything takes off, the harder you fall, the harder you take off! 2024, keep rushing! 🚀

★★★★

Day 1, 2024: Crossing the Seven Mile Bridge

☀️ When I woke up in the youth hostel, the sun was already high. This youth hostel is quite large and even has a swimming pool, and the water quality is quite clean.

🍽️ After breakfast, get ready to go back to Miami! The weather is very good today, and I can finally take a good look at the beautiful sea view of Highway 1.

🚗 Heading north, the first stop is the Seven-Mile Bridge!

🌍 This bridge is known as the "eighth wonder of the world", but to be honest, the "eighth wonder" of the world can be ranked in the rankings.

🌊 The Seven Mile Bridge is the longest of the 42 sea-crossing bridges in the Florida Keys. It was once a railroad bridge. Later, the hurricane came and the bridge collapsed. Now it has become a photo spot.

🛤️ The Old Flagler Railway Bridge still stands on the sea. It was once a railway passage, but now it has become a paradise for trails and fishermen. It is full of history!

🚘 The new bridge (now a highway bridge open to traffic) was built in 1982. Driving across the bridge, you will be surrounded by the endless blue sea on both sides, and the scenery is unparalleled!

📍 Park your car at Little Duck Key Wayside Park next to the Seven-Mile Bridge. This is a public boat dock and is also connected to the Florida Keys Overseas Heritage Trail. Many tourists walk here and there are many people fishing.

💨 The wind was super strong, but I couldn’t help but fly the drone

I’ve seen other people use drones to follow cars to shoot blockbusters before. It’s really cool, but with the wind, I don’t have the guts to do it. I’m content with simply flying.

Across the bridge, there is a heart-shaped island on the sea, Little Money Key - Cyjan Island. It sounds mysterious, but it is actually a private island. You cannot go to the island, so you can only look at it from a distance.

🔄 After retracing our steps, we parked the car near the Old Flagler Railway Bridge. How can seeing such a beautiful sea view once be enough?

⚠️ Note: There was a police car parked on the bridge. In less than five minutes, three speeding cars were caught. Real Thunder Law Enforcement!

🌊 The sea water here is so blue that it looks like it has been filtered. Standing by the old bridge, looking at the traffic on the new bridge and the blue sea and sky in the distance, I feel very comfortable.

🚗 On the way back to Miami, it is still a tropical sea road.

🏝️ A small town pops up from time to time along the way, with fishing boats and docks parked on the roadside. Even if you are just passing by, it exudes the unique sense of relaxation of Key West.

👨‍👩‍👧 I met a Chinese family living in Miami on the beach. They looked like parents coming to visit their daughter, and then we went on a self-driving trip together, enjoying the beauty of Key West like us.

🌞 Heading north, leaving Key West, this beautiful view of the Atlantic Ocean has already been engraved in my memory. 🚘💨

★★★★★

A day off in Miami: drop off the car, hang out, and catch up on work in the middle of the night!

🌞 Last day in Miami, eat and shop, and return the car at the same time

🚙 The return point is in Hialeah, a typical Hispanic community. Legend has it that there are a lot of car thefts and property crimes here. We parked the car and handed over the keys. We didn't dare to relax. We looked around to make sure there was nothing unusual, for fear that the car would be gone as soon as we turned around.

🚶‍♂️ After returning the car, we walked nervously through the community, quickly got on the light rail, and headed straight to downtown Miami.

🏞️ After two laps of the light rail, we arrived at the Miami River Walk

🚤 Here you can see the yachts rushing from the Miami River to the bay, with music and laughter coming and going. It is a completely different world from Hialeah.

🏝️ Opposite is Brickell Key, a small paradise for the rich in Miami. The cheapest houses on the island cost several million dollars.

🥢 High-end Chinese food - Hutong: You have to weigh your wallet before eating

🌆 Before dark, we needed to fill our stomachs, so we settled on a Chinese restaurant - Hutong.

💰 This meal refreshed my understanding of Chinese food and also refreshed my wallet balance.

🍽️ The environment is amazing, the presentation is exquisite, and the dishes are exquisite. Sure enough, Chinese food can be so high-end.

💸 We didn’t dare to order too much. After all, the price of each dish was quite shocking, and it felt like we had a Michelin-like atmosphere.

🚎 After dinner, I took a bus back to the B&B, thinking I could finally get a good sleep.

📞 In the middle of the night, the phone rang, and I saw it was "Guide" - I still couldn't escape asking for leave.

🖥️ He was going to another school and needed to draw pictures and do PPT, which was quite anxious (of course I only found out about this later).

😤 It’s quite magical realism when I think about it - if he interviews for a job, what does it have to do with me?

🖥️ In desperation, I turned on the computer and started working on it like a tiger. I continued to work until 2:25 in the morning, but I still didn't finish it.

Postscript

The journey continues, next stop: Orlando

🏰Orlando, FL

🌟 Although traveling with a computer is a bit frustrating, this is the reality, so we don’t have to worry about it anymore. It’s time to play and eat.

🎢 Next, we’re going to Orlando! This journey was more exciting, with scares and surprises, and I rode a lot of roller coasters.

🎡 Florida “really” doesn’t care for idlers, see you in Orlando!
