Run50 State 15 in Florida: Orlando Disney Marathon, with parks, characters, fireworks, and 26.2 miles of theme-park energy.

丨Florida丨

-Run50#15th State Marathon-

📍Location: Orlando, Florida

🎽Event: Walt Disney Dopey Challenge

Preface

After experiencing the 5K, 10K, and the “fake half marathon” that was shortened by the weather in the past few days, we finally came to the finale of the Disney Dopey Challenge: the full marathon (42.2km)!

After running this race, I not only officially unlocked Florida and became the 15th state on my American marathon map, but I could also collect three more medals at once: a full marathon medal, a Goofy Challenge medal (half marathon + full marathon), and a Dopey Challenge medal (four-day perfect attendance award).

The route map is provided. This trip is really a complete run through the park: starting from EPCOT, passing through Magic Kingdom, taking photos and checking in at Animal Kingdom, and finally passing by Hollywood Studios, and finally rushing back to EPCOT to complete the finish line. Not one of the four major Disney parks is missing.

Dopey Challenge Marathon @Arsenan

The race is still the "early morning wake-up series", starting at 5:30, Siqi sent me to the starting point as usual, thank you for the hard work 47 Sherpa.

But I heard that the start time will be moved up to 4:30 in 2026... This is very Disney. It’s the park during the day and the track in the early morning, without any delay.

★

Starting point outside EPCOT: start early in the morning

The starting point is on the highway outside EPCOT, with flashing lights, loud music and a warm atmosphere. Among the people passing by, I also saw the running legend "Brother Ni" flashing by.

I was wearing Arsenal's gold jersey, listening to the host's spoken English, practicing my listening skills, and waiting for the start.

We finally set off. Spectators on the roadside were shouting with lights and signs, volunteers kept cheering, and runners were running and jumping.

After the third mile of running, there was a team playing drums and a neat brass band. The rhythm is very exciting, like running into some kind of "Disney version of military parade".

At the 4th kilometer, I saw a familiar figure on the roadside, red hat, green clothes, tool belt, Handy Manny!

I rushed over, stood in line, and took a photo with him.

Around the 5th kilometer, I queued for the toilet for five minutes. The queue for the toilet can be considered one of the scenery on the track.

After recovering, we continued on the road and passed the Magic Kingdom toll booth. We could see the morning light starting to shine in the direction of the castle in the distance.

The sky is getting brighter, and the shadow of the runner is getting clearer and clearer. I know that the truly “fun” “Disney Castle” is coming.

★★

Miles 7~11: Magic Kingdom · Run into the big castle

Checking in at the 7th kilometer, Donald Duck cheered everyone on the street sign, and it was still not bright yet.

The section into Magic Kingdom is a surprising part. Ahead is a straight avenue with dense runners and rows of spectators. Everyone held fluorescent lights and slogans and cheered and cheered all the way.

Siqi was also in the crowd, but I didn’t look at him because there were too many people.

Turning into Main Street USA, the whole street is lit up with lights and there is the sound of Disney music. It has a contrasting feeling of traveling from the highway in the early morning to a fairy tale town.

Runners took out their mobile phones to take photos. At the end of the main street was Cinderella Castle under the early morning light.

I also took a lot of photos. I’m not a Disney fan, but this big castle is really pretty!

Go around the carousel and run under the castle. The sound of footsteps and cheers echoed in the arch. I looked up and saw that the lights were still changing colors.

Running out of the arch is another world. Frontierland, Liberty Square... streets with different styles are like visiting ten countries in one go.

No one here is chasing PB (except Brother Ni). Everyone is taking photos, posting on Moments, and making phone calls. Some people jumped forward, while others walked in circles while talking on the phone.

I didn’t stop for long, but I still posted it on Moments, proving that I was “here for the trip”

★★★

Miles 12-17: Animal Kingdom·Animal Kingdom

This journey is a bit long. After coming out of Magic Kingdom, the team entered a quiet road.

There are woods on both sides, the air is moist, and the sounds of footsteps and breathing are intertwined. Occasionally, we pass by one or two photography spots, where everyone lines up to take photos and take a breather.

I also joined the group of photographers on this stretch of road. Vanellope von Schweetz, the genius racing driver in "Wreck-It Ralph", was right on the side of the road.

She looked super cool, and her eyes seemed to say: "Run faster, you noobs." Of course I couldn't be cowardly, so I took a photo with her and continued without looking back.

Running further, I finally arrived at the main entrance of Animal Kingdom, and the excitement suddenly returned.

The first thing you see when you run into the park is the Tree of Life, which stands tall in the middle of the pool. The tree trunk is covered with animal totems. It is really cool! Disney can actually make nature look so artistic.

The theme areas in the park are constantly changing. From Asia to Africa, there are colorful flags all the way, which reminds me of Yubeng Hiking.

I ran near the "Everest Roller Coaster" and thought about doing a handsome "man's jump" in front of the Himalayas.

As a result, I dropped my Action 2 and it was damaged on the spot. But the photo was taken. It would be better if the photo behind me could be removed.

I also borrowed the restroom in the park along the way and "emptied" it properly. This is the hidden benefit of the Disney Marathon. You don’t have to squat on the side of the road or worry about toilet paper.

At the end of this section of the road, I met Princess Sofia the First. She was standing there quietly in a light purple dress. Many people ran by in a hurry, but I stopped and took a photo with her. After all, I am familiar with Sophia Cathedral.

Passing by the band conductor climbing the ladder, we soon reached mile 18. I'm not a fast runner, so it was pretty easy.

★★★★

Miles 18~24: Hollywood Studios · Hollywood Studios!

It’s 18 miles. The heavy rain has stopped, the air is humid, and the soles of runners’ shoes are still making a “squeaking” sound. On the way into the park, Disney characters gathered together during this section.

Although the road in this section is covered with gray asphalt and we haven’t reached Hollywood Studios yet, the density of characters is very high.

First came Qiqi and Didi, wearing colorful polka-dot vests, one with rolled-up sleeves, and the other with arms folded across his chest. I was sandwiched in the middle and surrounded, as if I had joined their squirrel brotherhood;

Immediately after turning into "Frozen", Anna and Elsa stood in front of the icicles, one clenching their fists in encouragement and the other smiling, as if they had just participated in a beauty pageant;

Further forward, there are Officer Judy and Fox Nick, who have traveled directly from "Animal City". Judy crossed her arms and looked serious; Nick leaned against me, looking like an old man.

There was no kilometers running at all, and the whole process was a "photo sprint". I like this kind of social intensity!

Elsa, Anna and Olaf were standing in front of the 19th kilometer sign. They looked very relaxed. I was actually not tired either, after all, I had been queuing up for a group photo.

Further ahead, Fancy Nancy, wearing a rainbow skirt, pulled me into a temporary ballet hero;

The little vampire Vampirina raised her fists and shouted "Go!". I could only accompany "Gogogo" for fear of being "sucked" away the last breath of strength by her.

Not long after I continued running, a small talk show booth suddenly popped up on the left hand side. It was very interesting.

We soon entered the inner campus of Hollywood Studios. As soon as you look up, you will see the iconic Palm Avenue, surrounded by old Hollywood buildings full of cinematic feel.

Running in this area, I feel like I have become the protagonist in a Disney movie, surrounded by strangers in the audience who are constantly applauding and cheering, and various cartoon characters lining up to take photos.

On the boardwalk by the lake, I met Bruno from "Full House", the Bruno we won't talk about. He was wearing a green cloak and his movements were exaggerated.

After running not far, I met Mira again, the little girl from "Royal Detective". She was wearing an Indian-style blue and gold coat. She looked more like a serious runner than me. She stood upright and had a bright smile;

At the end of this section, after leaving Hollywood Studios, we ran along a lakeside plank road. The wooden boardwalk creaks when you step on it, with the sparkling lake on one side and the colorful Disney Hotel flags blowing in the wind on the other. It's very comfortable!

These seven miles are not short, but because of the company of these characters, it always feels like time flies by. The sky gradually cleared up, the sun shone down, and everything became light again.

★★★★★

Miles 24-26: Epcot finish line sprint

At mile 24, Luisa pumped her fists and had a "you can do it" look on her face. I nodded, that’s a must!

The boardwalk creaked underfoot, and more and more spectators gathered. They held signs: "Registration was harder than this." Indeed, compared to running, registration seems to be the most difficult part.

After entering EPCOT, the window to the world unfolds: the five-story pagoda in Japan, the wooden church in Norway, the Chaoyang Gate in China, the pyramids in Mexico, and the brick houses in Germany.

I ran over step by step, like flipping through a thick photo album in order, except that the photos would move.

At mile 25, Goofy was wearing a yellow protective suit and gestured "Come on" to me. Then there's Star-Lord. He asked me if I was a "Guardian of the Galaxy." I said, I am the guardian of 42 kilometers, and I will only guard this section of the road today.

The end of the world is not a steep slope, but a choir. Wearing bright yellow robes, they stood in front of EPCOT's "Future Earth" and sang, the lyrics unintelligible but the rhythm just right.

The moment I crossed the line, it was indeed quite relaxing. It took less than 5 hours, because I had been queuing up to take photos during these 42 kilometers. I couldn’t compare with Brother Ni, but I definitely took more photos than him!

Siqi was waiting for me at the finish line and used a 360 to take a video of me crossing the finish line!

It was quite satisfying to receive three medals at once. The quality of the medals was also very good, and the designs were quite happy. One piece says 26.2 miles, and Mickey and Minnie are jumping on it, which is for the main marathon. One is Goofy's swing, marked 39.3 miles, which represents running a half marathon and a full marathon in two days.

The last piece is Dopey, 48.6 miles, four races in four days, challenging to pass. Hanging on my chest, it's heavy, banging, and has great texture.

After returning to our accommodation, we ordered takeout. Burger + French fries, a "real" American meal!

We have a big plan in the afternoon. We want to really go to Magic Kingdom to have a look and replenish Siqi. It’s a bit of a loss not to go to a single garden. I can also take the opportunity to take pictures of the medals!

Postscript

Medals jingle all the way into Magic Kingdom

In the afternoon we went to Magic Kingdom. This is our second time here. The first time was a marathon in the morning. There were no events other than running.

At this moment, I was walking down the main street with my medal hanging on it, jingling like wind chimes. When I turned around, I realized that everyone was doing the same! They are all horse racers!

The main street is full of official Disney photographers, taking super professional photos.

I bought a PhotoPass with an Indian buddy a few days ago. It only costs a few dozen dollars. It’s a 4-day competition + download photos of the park. It’s a sure profit.

In the afternoon, there was a Bruno-themed parade, and the whole crowd sang “Don’t talk about Bruno~”. We also stood on the street and swayed along, and the atmosphere was overflowing.

I lined up a few rides, and the overall feeling was more childlike, a children's version of a roller coaster. Universal Studios partygoers may not find it exciting enough, but I hear Animal Kingdom’s “Himalayas” is pretty good!

As the sky darkened in the evening and the castle was lit up, we found a spot among the crowd to wait for the fireworks.

The fireworks at night were great, with classic soundtrack + castle projection, accompanied by "Let It Go" and "A Whole New World". The sound bass was very strong, and the lights swept across the crowd, making everyone's face light up.

Although the race does not include tickets, the ticket is quite worth buying. Running and the fairy tale world are perfectly connected.

To be honest, I don’t have much interest in Disney, I’m not obsessed with Mickey, and I don’t watch princess movies. Four runs in four days, 48.6 miles. There are a lot of entertainment elements, but not many hardcore places.

However, on my Run50 map, I actually painted the 15th state marathon.

There are still 35 states short of 50.

- The end -
