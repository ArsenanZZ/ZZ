Principles & Buyer's Guide

硬水富含钙镁矿物质，会导致水垢积聚、皮肤发硬。软水机通过离子交换过程，彻底替换原水中的钙镁离子，延长涉水电器寿命，带来柔和亲肤的洗浴体验。 Hard water is rich in calcium and magnesium minerals, causing scale build-up and dry skin. Water softeners use an ion exchange process to replace these minerals, protecting appliances and providing a luxurious, skin-friendly washing experience.

Ion Exchange Principle

离子交换：微观软化原理 Ion Exchange: Microscopic Softening

软水机核心是食品级强酸性阳离子交换树脂，它是带负电荷的球状颗粒，表面吸附有钠离子（Na⁺）。当硬度离子（Ca²⁺, Mg²⁺）流经树脂时，它们与钠离子进行位置交换，完成水质软化。 The core of a softener is food-grade strong acid cation exchange resin. These negative charged beads hold sodium (Na⁺) ions. As hard water (Ca²⁺/Mg²⁺) passes through the resin, it binds to the beads and releases sodium, softening the water.

01

硬水流入，发生置换 Hard Water Inflow

自来水从树脂罐顶部压入，其中的钙镁离子（硬度来源）与树脂表面的钠离子接触。由于树脂对带双电荷的 Ca²⁺、Mg²⁺ 吸附力更强，钠离子被“替换”下，进入水中，钙镁则被牢牢吸附。 Hard water enters the resin tank. Because the resin beads have a stronger affinity for Ca²⁺ and Mg²⁺ than for single-charged Na⁺, the hard minerals kick the sodium off and stick to the beads.

02

树脂饱和，软化失效 Resin Saturation

随着通水量增加，树脂表面所有的交换位置逐渐被钙镁离子占满，此时树脂达到“饱和”状态（即上方模拟器中的饱和度达到 100%），机器无法继续软化水质，水龙头重新排出硬水。 Over time, all exchange sites on the resin are occupied by calcium and magnesium. The resin is 'saturated' (100% saturation in the simulator above) and can no longer soften the water.

03

高浓度盐洗，置换还原 Salt Brine Regeneration

此时，控制阀会自动启动“再生”程序，将高浓度的食盐水（NaCl）注入罐体。海量的 Na⁺ 离子强行把树脂上的 Ca²⁺、Mg²⁺ 顶替下来，并将其排入下水道，树脂重新装满钠离子，恢复软化能力。 To restore capacity, the valve injects highly concentrated sodium chloride brine (salt water). The massive surge of Na⁺ forces the calcium/magnesium off the beads and flushes them to the drain, resetting the resin.

Regeneration Technology

顺流再生 vs 逆流再生技术对比 Co-current vs Counter-current Regeneration

软水机的再生技术直接决定了后期的用盐与用水成本。逆流（上流式）再生在节水省盐上相比顺流（下流式）具有绝对优势。 The regeneration method determines salt and water efficiency. Upflow (Counter-current) regeneration saves significant resources compared to traditional Downflow (Co-current).

↓ 顺流再生 (Co-Current / Downflow) Co-current / Downflow

盐水流动方向与正常制水水流相同（自上而下）。再生时，最上层吸附硬度最高的树脂会先接触饱和盐水。被替换出来的钙镁离子会向下流动，被强行推过中下层较干净的树脂，极易在底部树脂形成二次污染（硬度迁移）。整个过程耗盐多、废水多。 Brine flows in the same direction as service water (downward). The heavily saturated top resin layer meets fresh brine first. Displaced hardness ions are pushed down through cleaner bottom resin, causing secondary hardness migration. Requires more salt and rinse water.

  • 耗盐量：高 (约 150g NaCl / 升树脂)Salt usage: High (~150g salt per liter resin)
  • 再生废水：高 (约 10-12 升 / 升树脂)Wastewater: High (~10-12L water per liter resin)
  • 应用品牌：中低端入门机型，机械老式阀体Applications: Economy models, older mechanical valves

↑ 逆流再生 (Counter-Current / Upflow) Counter-current / Upflow

盐水流动方向与制水方向相反（从底部向上流动）。最干净的底端树脂先接触最纯净的饱和盐水，获得极高的还原精度。被置换下来的钙镁离子被直接从顶部排出罐体，不会二次污染较干净的滤料。这能节省高达 30% 盐量和 45% 的废水。 Brine flows in the opposite direction of service water (upward from bottom). The cleanest bottom resin meets pure brine first, ensuring optimal restoration. Displaced ions are pushed straight out the top, saving up to 30% salt and 45% wastewater.

  • 耗盐量：低 (约 90g NaCl / 升树脂)Salt usage: Low (~90g salt per liter resin)
  • 再生废水：低 (约 6-7 升 / 升树脂)Wastewater: Low (~6-7L water per liter resin)
  • 应用品牌：怡口高端、克莱克盘阀、润新高效逆流系列Applications: EcoWater, Clack, Runxin advanced upflow series

Internal Mechanics

软水阀头内部结构剖析 Control Valve Head Internal Anatomy

为什么一个阀头能决定整台机器的寿命？让我们拆开阀体，看一看隐藏在控制阀里面的精密零件及其运作原理。 Why does the control valve head determine the entire machine's lifespan? Let's dissect the valve body and look at its internal precision parts.

A

驱动电机与减速齿轮组 Drive Motor & Gear Assembly

控制阀顶部的步进或直流电机，配合一组减速齿轮，精确控制内部活塞平移或瓷盘旋转的角度。定位偏差必须小于1毫米，否则会导致流道错位。 A stepper or DC motor coupled with precision gears. It moves the piston or rotates the ceramic disc to precise alignments. Deviation must be under 1mm.

B

控制活塞与格栅套件 (Spacer Stack & Piston) Piston and Spacer Stack

活塞套有多组橡胶圈，阀体内则套有一组固定塑料格栅套。随着活塞在不同行程间抽插，格栅的进出口被选择性封堵或连通，完成流道转换。 A Teflon-coated piston with seals moves through a stack of stationary plastic spacers. Piston strokes connect or seal specific ports to redirect water.

C

文丘里吸盐喷射器 (Venturi Injector) Venturi Injector

包含微细喷嘴和喉管。当高速冲洗水流经过时，在吸盐口产生强烈的真空负压（文丘里效应），将盐箱中的饱和盐水吸起。 High-velocity water flows through a micro nozzle and throat, generating a vacuum to draw concentrated brine from the salt tank.

D

霍尔流量计 (Turbine Flow Meter) Hall-Effect Flow Turbine

出水侧安装有磁性叶轮与霍尔传感器。出水流过时叶轮旋转，产生脉冲信号发送给主板，精确累积用水量以计算下次再生剩余吨数。 A magnetic turbine on the outlet spins when water flows. A Hall sensor counts pulses to track household water consumption and trigger regeneration.

E

定位 Cam 凸轮与光电定位板 Optical Cam & Position Sensor

轴上装有开缺口定位轮。光电传感器监测凸轮缺口的位置，从而反馈给芯片，让电机在活塞或瓷盘精准到达特定再生位置时停止。 A notched cam spins with the shaft. Optical sensors identify the notches to tell the circuit board exactly when the mechanism has reached the targeted cycle position.
